# Study and company watchlist

Checked 2026-09-11. This is a research snapshot, not a current appointment or an individual treatment recommendation. Dates below distinguish source updates from the date checked. No recurring monitor is configured.

## WIND-PSC — University of Alberta

**Status:** Observational cohort; Edmonton recruiting. **Source date:** 2026-09-08.

Collects clinical, imaging, blood and symptom data during usual care. No experimental drug is assigned.

**Watch next:** Track site enrollment and new observational findings. This study assigns no investigational treatment.

Sources: [Source 1](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06297993) · [Source 2](https://pscpartners.org/research/wind-psc/)

## Elafibranor — ELASCOPE / Ipsen

**Status:** Phase 3 PSC trial recruiting; 350 participants planned. **Source date:** 2026-08-31.

ELMWOOD randomized 68 adults for 12 weeks. ALP change versus placebo: −35.3 percentage points at 80 mg and −54.7 at 120 mg. Safety was the primary endpoint. Adverse-event rates: 68.2%, 78.3%, 69.6% respectively; long-term benefit remains unproven.

**Watch next:** Track Canadian site additions and phase 3 clinical-outcome results. US eligibility for Canadian residents remains unconfirmed.

Sources: [Source 1](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07387549) · [Source 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40350321/)

## Norucholic acid — NUC-5 / Dr. Falk Pharma

**Status:** Phase 3 96-week results reported; Access route unresolved. **Source date:** 2025-05-07.

Sponsor report: combined ALP improvement plus no histological worsening in 15.1% versus 4.2% on placebo. Histology improved in 25.2% versus 10.5%. Adverse-event rates described as similar. These results do not establish a survival benefit.

**Watch next:** Track full publication, longer follow-up and actual regulatory/access decisions. An old registry entry does not establish recruitment.

Sources: [Source 1](https://drfalkpharma.com/fileadmin/FP_HQ/newsroom/20250507_NCA/DrFalkPharma_PressRelease_NCA-StudyResult-in-PSC_FIN.pdf) · [Source 2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03872921)

## LB-P8 — LISCure Biosciences

**Status:** Phase 2 randomized trial; Recruiting in registry; listing is old. **Source date:** 2025-10-24.

Live microbial candidate. The trial evaluates safety and ALP change over up to 24 weeks. No placebo-controlled human efficacy result was identified in this review; laboratory/mouse findings remain preliminary.

**Watch next:** Confirm current recruitment because the record is old; watch for randomized human efficacy and safety results.

Sources: [Source 1](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06699121) · [Source 2](https://clinicaltrials.ucdavis.edu/trial/NCT06699121)

## Volixibat — VISTAS / Mirum

**Status:** Phase 2b results reported; Active, not recruiting. **Source date:** 2026-08-26.

Sponsor reported an improvement in itch in VISTAS. This supports a symptom-treatment hypothesis; prevention of PSC progression has not been established.

**Watch next:** Track the next protocol and regulatory decisions. Symptom relief does not establish slower PSC progression.

Sources: [Source 1](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04663308) · [Source 2](https://ir.mirumpharma.com/news/news-details/2026/Mirum-Pharmaceuticals-Announces-Primary-Endpoint-Met-in-VISTAS-Study-of-Volixibat-in-Patients-with-Primary-Sclerosing-Cholangitis/default.aspx) · [Source 3](https://ir.mirumpharma.com/news/news-details/2026/Mirum-Pharmaceuticals-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-and-Provides-Business-Update/default.aspx)

## RTY-406 — Rectify Pharmaceuticals

**Status:** Phase 1 according to sponsor; PSC-directed programme. **Source date:** 2026-08-27.

Small molecule modulating ABCB4 and BSEP bile transport proteins. Reported improvements in inflammation/fibrosis come from a mouse model. No human PSC efficacy result identified.

**Watch next:** Track registered human trials, safety results and enrollment of people with PSC. A mouse finding is not human efficacy.

Sources: [Source 1](https://rectifypharma.com/press_release/rectify-pharmaceuticals-presents-discovery-of-clinical-candidate-rty-406-a-novel-disease-modifying-therapy-for-the-treatment-of-psc-at-acs-fall-2026-meeting/)

## AX-0811 — ProQR Therapeutics

**Status:** Phase 1 healthy volunteers; Adjacent cholestatic-disease programme. **Source date:** 2026-09-09.

First participant dosed September 9. An RNA-editing candidate targeting NTCP. The current announcement emphasizes biliary atresia; PSC efficacy and a PSC-patient trial are unconfirmed.

**Watch next:** Track human target engagement and any PSC-specific development announcement. This is currently an adjacent-disease lead.

Sources: [Source 1](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/09/3358547/0/en/proqr-announces-first-participant-dosed-in-phase-1-study-of-ax-0811-and-virtual-investor-and-analyst-educational-event-on-september-30-2026.html)
