# Fine-mapping provenance questions

Prepared September 12, 2026 for possible review by the Goode et al. study authors or archive maintainers. **Unsent.** No researcher has been contacted about this audit.

Context: we are building a small public-data regulatory-model comparison for PSC. We downloaded the [Zenodo fine-mapping release](https://zenodo.org/records/11143561) and preserved its original values. The complete [audit](../reports/psc-finemap-and-comparison.md) and [dataset-level table](../reports/psc-finemap-datasets.csv) show the checks. We would appreciate clarification of which files correspond to the published analyses:

1. **Run/version alignment.** All 26 archived logs say FINEMAP v1.1; the paper's Methods say v1.3. Sixteen GWAS configuration files contain multiple-variant configurations although their logs specify `n-causal-max: 1`. The distribution of the number of causal variants differs between logs and saved configurations in 23 of 25 comparable datasets. The SNP probabilities and configuration-summed marginals nevertheless agree within rounding in all 25. Are updated logs or exact run manifests available?
2. **Published credible-set membership.** Could you share the per-signal variant lists, confidence level, set-construction procedure and associated model run for Tables 1 and 2? Under the archived singleton UBASH3A model we obtain seven variants at 95% cumulative probability; the table lists five. We have kept those results separate and have not assumed the table uses our reconstruction rule.
3. **Identifiers and study alleles.** Is there a mapping file or shareable `.z` table linking `kgp`, `SNP_A`, coordinate-only and semicolon-joined identifiers to build, REF/ALT and effect alleles? For example, the shortlisted rs4147359 is currently multiallelic in Ensembl, and we cannot identify its study-specific allele pair from the released SNP table. CD28 signal 2 also lists rs231799 at 0.17 in Table 1, versus 0.0004 for the literal archive record.
4. **Additional/missing files and sample scope.** What distinguishes the two PRKD2-region monocyte runs, including the empty non-`_2CV` configuration file? Are the extra GPR35, HDAC7 and SIK2 archive regions from the final analysis? Our subsequent source audit finds that Ji et al. 2017 Supplementary Table 1 has an Omni subset of 2,170 cases and 9,216 controls, totaling 11,386. Does this explain the GWAS-log field, and which genotyping-platform variants and participants actually entered the archived fine-mapping runs? Could you share the allele-coded original association inputs, per-variant sample scope, signed in-sample LD/covariance, and matching run manifest? This count match alone does not resolve the log/configuration differences above.

These questions concern reuse and reproducibility. They are not an allegation of invalid results, a request for patient records or a clinical consultation. An author response would be recorded as new evidence alongside the existing source values.
