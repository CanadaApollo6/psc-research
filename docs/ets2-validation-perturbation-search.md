# Independent ETS2 and MEK perturbation search

Search date: **2026-09-12**. This is a metadata and published-narrative search with preserved original [selection rules](../config/ets2-validation-rules.json) and [validation protocol](ets2-validation-protocol.md). Future analyses follow the [effective amendment](../config/ets2-validation-amended-rules.json). The candidate inventory is in [ets2-validation-perturbation-candidates.csv](../data/derived/ets2-validation-perturbation-candidates.csv), and source bytes and hashes are in the [source manifest](../config/ets2-validation-perturbation-sources.json).

The bounded search found **no eligible Tier A direct ETS2 perturbation study**. It found no Tier B study that satisfies all frozen gates. The strongest human Tier B biological evidence is GSE84161: five donor-like paired MEK-inhibitor and vehicle sets in primary blood monocytes, but the public measurement is RMA microarray intensity and its replicate-to-donor key is not separately audited. GSE39745 has three named donor pairs with U0126 and vehicle, but is Agilent microarray data. GSE123573 has human monocyte trametinib RNA-seq, but only one complete treated/control RNA pair remains and its files are RSEM expected counts. These remain useful leads for a separate array or count calibration amendment; none was selected and `ready_for_inference` remains false.

## Search coverage and stopping rule

The primary-source search used NCBI GEO, PubMed and the public BioStudies API. Queries and raw responses are cached under `work/ets2-independent-search/` and linked from the source manifest.

- GEO direct ETS2 query: `ETS2 AND (knockdown OR knockout OR CRISPR OR shRNA OR siRNA OR overexpression)`, 151 hits returned after requesting `retmax=200`. The accession-level result included GSE175685, GSE12863 and non-myeloid or non-transcriptomic hits.
- GEO myeloid ETS2 queries: `ETS2 AND macrophage`, 11 hits with the first 10 returned in the initial snapshot; and `ETS2 AND monocyte`, 8 hits. The initial macrophage result was page-limited by `retmax=10`; the omission is preserved rather than presented as exhaustive coverage. A 16-accession union was summarized for triage.
- GEO MEK query: `(MEK OR MAP2K OR trametinib OR selumetinib OR PD0325901 OR PD184352 OR U0126 OR cobimetinib OR binimetinib OR CI-1040) AND (macrophage OR monocyte OR myeloid)`, 546 hits, first 500 summarized (`retmax=500`). The remaining 46 hits were not fetched; later metadata triage focused on the human primary, recent, and direct macrophage/monocyte records among the first 500.
- PubMed date-bounded transcriptome query, publication years 2000–2026: `ETS2[Title/Abstract] AND (macrophage OR monocyte OR myeloid) AND (RNA-seq OR transcriptom* OR expression)`, 71 hits. Direct-perturbation query: `ETS2[Title/Abstract] AND (knockdown OR knockout OR overexpression OR CRISPR) AND (macrophage OR monocyte OR myeloid)`, 30 hits. Recent abstracts were checked for 2024–2026 study designs and public-accession clues; recent hits were observational, in silico, qPCR/protein/flow, metabolic-treatment or non-myeloid model studies, or reviews.
- BioStudies API page-1 checks: exact `"ETS2"` returned 985 total hits with 20 displayed; expanded `ETS2 macrophage` returned 34,199 with 20 displayed; exact `"trametinib" "monocyte"` returned 12,144 with 20 displayed; exact `"MEK" "monocyte"` returned 15,487 with 20 displayed. These totals are marked non-exact by the API and were not treated as exhaustive. Displayed ETS2 hits were fibroblast, cancer, source-study or unrelated studies; the relevant source-study hit is S-EPMC11168933, the Stankey paper.

No access-controlled data were requested. No raw sequencing reads were downloaded. The final search boundary is this date and these query/page limits; an eligible study may exist outside them.

## Vetted candidates

### Direct ETS2 model evidence

**GSE175685 — Tier D, highest-priority model context.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE175685) describes a whole-RNA-seq THP-1 ORF screen. The metadata explicitly contains ETS2 samples GSM5343869, GSM5343870 and GSM5343871, titled `THP-1 ORF ETS2 rep1-3`, with `overexpressed gene: ETS2`; it also contains eleven `THP-1 EMPTY` controls GSM5343992–GSM5344002. The ETS2 samples use infection date 2016-01-13, 18-day culture and batch B. Same-date empty controls include 15-day culture, with additional date and batch variation. The only public expression file is a 16,295,158-byte normalized FPKM table; sample supplementary fields are `NONE`. This verifies a direct ETS2 arm and a concrete model benchmark, but it has zero biological donors, duration/batch confounding and no raw integer count matrix. It cannot serve as Tier A. The linked [primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36155972/) is separate from Stankey, although exact donor overlap is unknown.

**GSE12863 — Tier D mouse direct-deletion context.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE12863) and [primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20145133/) describe conditional Ets2 deletion in PyMT mammary tumor-associated and normal remodeling macrophages. There is one RNA sample per genetic or tissue group, four array samples total, and a 299,362-byte processed series matrix. It is direct ETS2 biology but mouse, unreplicated at donor level and array-based.

### Human MEK evidence

**GSE84161 — Tier B biological lead, not frozen-count eligible.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE84161) states that primary human monocytes were purified from buffy coats from five donors, cultured with M-CSF, pretreated with MEK SMI at 0.31 uM or TPL2 SMI, and collected with or without LPS. Sample records GSM2228386–GSM2228415 form six conditions across replicate sets 1–5. The MEK SMI and vehicle arms are arranged as five donor-like pairs in both unstimulated and LPS conditions, but the metadata does not provide a separate donor identifier beyond the replicate-like labels. The exact MEK compound is not named. The public processed series matrix is a 3,504,880-byte GPL570 RMA intensity matrix with 19,944 probe-set rows per sample; raw array files are listed, but no integer gene counts are available. It is the strongest human Tier B biological design found by donor-like pair count and control structure, while the replicate-to-donor audit and array calibration remain open.

**GSE39745 — Tier B biological lead, not frozen-count eligible.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE39745) and [primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23430108/) describe CD14-positive monocytes from three healthy donors, differentiated for five days with GM-CSF and IL-4 into immature MDDC, then treated with U0126 or vehicle for one hour. U0126 and DMSO are paired for donors DC4, DC5 and DC7 at 4, 10 and 24 hours; duplicate arrays are separately labeled. The assay is an Agilent Human Whole Genome 4x44 microarray with a 6,243,937-byte processed series matrix and 45,220 rows per sample. No raw integer gene counts exist. It has three named donor pairs per time point, but its log2 or normalized array quantity needs a separate prespecified calibration.

**GSE123573 — Tier B RNA-seq lead, one pair only.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE123573) and [primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31644909/) describe primary CD14-positive human monocytes exposed to GM-CSF or M-CSF. The RNA series has 11 samples and uses RSEM expected counts after STAR mapping. The only explicit trametinib RNA pair is GSM3507333 `Tram_1GM` versus GSM3507331 `Cont_1GM`; the series says one Trametinib sample was excluded and no `Tram_2GM` RNA sample is present. The public per-sample files are 524,807 and 521,665 bytes, respectively. Because only one complete pair remains and the quantity is expected counts rather than a declared raw integer matrix, it fails the frozen B gate.

**GSE263046 — Tier D under frozen mixed-tissue rule, borderline B context.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE263046) and [primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38907342/) report matched pre/on-selumetinib cutaneous-neurofibroma biopsy sets from four individuals. Public 10x mRNA and ADT matrices include myeloid cells among Schwann, fibroblast, endothelial and other nuclei; the series archive is 97,351,680 bytes and a representative mRNA matrix is 14,667,707 bytes. Treatment labels are hashtag-demultiplexed within mixed-tissue libraries. The public UMI matrices are promising integer-count inputs, but exact donor-condition hash mapping, per-donor myeloid cell counts, and the completeness of each myeloid treatment stratum were not inspected. Mixed tissue and in situ clinical treatment place it in Tier D under the frozen rules; it cannot replace a sorted primary-human myeloid experiment without a separate single-cell calibration and freeze.

### Mouse and observational context

- **GSE134486 — Tier D.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE134486) and [primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31926447/) describe trametinib versus DMSO in LPS-challenged murine peritoneal macrophages. Three numbered LD samples and three LT samples are suggestive of three pairs; the public featureCounts table is 230,645 bytes. Mouse species keeps it contextual.
- **GSE123512 — Tier D.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE123512) and [primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31507609/) describe U0126 with or without R848 in WT or Irf1-null mouse BMDMs. Ten array samples have M1/M2 labels but no explicit donor identities; the processed series matrix is 1,481,907 bytes.
- **GSE264420 — Tier D.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE264420) and [primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39620221/) describe Nor1–7 and PH1–7 spleen macrophage observation groups. The series narrative mentions in vitro rafametinib, but no inhibitor-labeled RNA sample is present in this accession. GEO labels the samples human while their processing metadata says mm10 or GrCM38; the public table is RPKM and 6,170,164 bytes. It is not a paired MEK perturbation dataset.

### Reuse exclusion

**GSE229472 — Tier R.** The [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE229472) and [Stankey primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38839969/) describe an MPRA in primary human macrophages from eight healthy individuals. It contains barcode and collapsed-construct counts rather than gene-by-sample transcriptomics and is the same source study used for the ETS2 program. It is retained only to make source reuse and selection overlap explicit.

## Independence, measurement and readiness

GSE175685, GSE84161, GSE39745, GSE123573 and GSE263046 have distinct study records and contributor groups from Stankey, but public records do not establish individual-level donor overlap for the human donor studies. GSE175685 is a cell-line model, so a primary-donor replication claim is inapplicable. Different accession numbers are recorded as separate study provenance, not proof of unrelated donors. No derivation or selection reuse was identified for the separate-study leads in the inspected sources. GSE229472 remains the explicit Stankey source-study reuse exception.

The inventory records public file availability and sizes without reading expression outcomes. The frozen raw-count requirement remains decisive: normalized FPKM, RMA or Agilent intensity, expected counts with unresolved integer status, and MPRA barcode counts cannot be substituted for a gene-by-sample raw integer matrix. No program mapping, expression aggregation, effect estimate, score, or directional comparison was performed. A Tier B array or borderline single-cell follow-up would require a dataset-specific amendment and freeze before any numerical inspection.

## Recent literature exclusions

The 2024–2026 PubMed triage included [Stankey 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38839969/), [the 2024 selumetinib cNF single-nucleus study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38907342/), [the 2024 human-spleen MEK-ERK-c-MYC study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39620221/), and recent ETS2 macrophage papers such as [the 2025 UC shRNA study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40708063/) and [the 2026 exosomal-ETS2 study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42530712/). The recent direct ETS2 papers use cell models or qPCR, Western, flow, in silico perturbation, or observational/single-cell tissue analyses and did not expose a new donor-annotated direct ETS2 bulk count accession. The cNF study is retained above as the closest human MEK single-cell context. This is an exclusion record, not a claim that no future dataset can exist.
