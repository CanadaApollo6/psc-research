# PSC blood: cross-cohort replication protocol

Written September 15, 2026 before new gene-wise case-control effects. Source study narratives and their published benchmark genes are already known. This is a new public-data reanalysis, not a registered or untouched validation study.

## Question and choice

Which whole-blood RNA associations with PSC reproduce across the Norwegian RNA-seq cohort and a separate Polish microarray cohort, and which also differ from bowel and other cholestatic disease controls? This path is preferred over another regulatory model sweep or a four-donor generic stimulation study because it adds directly relevant disease observations and an independent study family.

The main result is **cross-cohort gene-level reproducibility**, not classifier accuracy, a diagnostic biomarker, a cell-intrinsic effect or a causal PSC mechanism. A null or nonspecific result is informative. Published IFIT1/G0S2 and the project's existing five candidates are benchmarks, not newly discovered genes.

## Design known before effects

- GSE177044 contains two countries/case-control cohorts. Norway provides 220 PSC cases (177 with UC, 43 without) and 77 controls on plates PS449–PS453. Germany provides 495 UC cases and 243 controls on other plates. The primary analysis uses Norway only, adjusting for age, recorded sex and sequencing plate. No pooled PSC-versus-UC classifier or direct contrast is used.
- GSE119600 supplies 370 deposited arrays. The replication arm retains all 275 source-labeled adults: PSC 45, control 47, UC 45, PBC 90, CD 48. It excludes 95 source-labeled pediatric IBD samples before effects. The adult label is a source field, not independent proof that every participant was at least 18; paper age ranges/counts have discrepancies.
- These are separate study families and recruitment countries, not identity-verified disjoint participants. The RNA-seq paper already reports weak classifier performance in an external PSC dataset; the Polish cohort is that previously used external dataset. No new diagnostic validation claim is made.
- The Polish public sample records lack exact age, sex, treatment and batch covariates. PBC recruitment is female-only and disease-group ages differ in the paper. Disease comparisons therefore describe unadjusted total-blood associations, not disease-intrinsic specificity. Shared samples across contrasts are not independent replications.
- The primary analysis includes every eligible deposited sample. No additional outlier exclusion is chosen by effect, expected direction or classifier performance. Historical source exclusion statements without exact sample identifiers remain unresolved; no sample identity is guessed.

## Planned measurements

RNA-seq: explicit raw-count identities and complete sample joins; retain genes with at least 10 counts in at least 30 of 297 selected samples (ceiling of 10%, fixed before effects); PyDESeq2 negative-binomial model with diagnosis, age, sex and plate; source counts unchanged; no automatic replacement of outlier counts. Request parametric dispersion; the pinned PyDESeq2 built-in mean-dispersion fallback is allowed and must be recorded if used. Ratio normalization requires at least one eligible all-positive gene; no unplanned normalization fallback is allowed. Preserve PyDESeq2's own adjusted P field separately; the primary BH family includes all count-eligible genes, using 1 only as the correction input for unavailable Wald tests while leaving source P values unavailable. An initial implementation discussion considered 10 people; the final 30-person rule follows the source's 10% precedent and is chosen before reading new effects. Retain every unavailable statistical result and its reason. Genomewide BH correction is applied without selecting the tested family from favored genes. A log2(CPM+1) HC3 linear-model sensitivity (asymptotic-normal inference, full unfiltered library sums) and leave-one-plate-out direction checks examine dependence on modeling choices and a single plate. A prespecified three-level-diagnosis OLS model also reports PSC-alone versus control and PSC-with-UC versus control, with the same age, sex and plate covariates. These shared-control subgroup sensitivities are not independent replication. Exact filter and software settings are fixed in the execution plan before effects.

Array: exact GPL10558 probe-to-Entrez mapping. Multi-gene or missing mappings remain excluded with counts and reasons. Transform documented positive linear normalized intensities to log2, then take each sample's median across all uniquely mapped probes for a gene. Do not select a probe by expression variance or association. Retain all mapped genes and all eligible adults; no new quantile normalization fitted using disease labels. Pairwise Welch comparisons (Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom) are PSC-control (primary), PSC-UC, PSC-PBC and PSC-CD. Separately reported two-group OLS HC3 uses asymptotic-normal inference; its mean difference matches Welch but its SE does not. It is a sensitivity, not a second replication. Exact handling of any structural/nonpositive source values must be frozen before effects, not patched after seeing a candidate.

Gene identities: reuse the complete pinned Ensembl 116 relationship table from the prior GSE84161 mapping audit, recomputing reciprocal one-to-one Ensembl–Entrez eligibility across its entire human source universe. Preserve original IDs, source gene labels, version-removal transformations and unmapped/ambiguous cases. Do not guess aliases or combine multiple RNA genes because they share a symbol.

## Replication and complete reporting

For each eligible shared gene, the primary conjunction P is the larger of the Norwegian PSC-control and Polish PSC-control two-sided P values when signs agree; otherwise it is 1. If either source test is unavailable, the conjunction uses 1 and reports the original test as unavailable, not a biological null. BH correction uses the complete declared shared eligible gene family. This intersection-union rule does not count the two platforms as directly comparable effect scales; no fold-change meta-analysis is performed.

The secondary disease-control support family additionally requires the Polish PSC-UC sign and P; a separate broader family additionally requires PSC-PBC and PSC-CD. Use the maximum constituent P with concordant signs, then BH within each full declared family. These correlated contrasts are not independent experiments. Both per-study results and all conjunction outcomes are retained. BH error control depends on its cross-gene dependence assumptions; these are not established by this dataset. Benjamini–Yekutieli correction, which permits arbitrary dependence for valid constituent P values, will be reported for all three conjunction families. It is a prespecified sensitivity, not a substitute chosen after observing the BH result.

A replicated candidate still may reflect blood-cell composition, age, sex, medication, disease stage or inflammation rather than a cell-intrinsic mechanism. No absent association proves no disease role. No treatment intervention follows directly from an expression direction.

## Completion boundary

Deliver immutable source/sample and execution manifests, all aggregate gene-level results, ID/coverage and model QC, a plot, independent numerical checks and offline replay. Report all earlier and newly found limitations. If structural qualification fails, identify the exact missing input without treating it as a biological result. Further organoid/liver-cell or perturbation work requires a separate prospective plan based on this result.
