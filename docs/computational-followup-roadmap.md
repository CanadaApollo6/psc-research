## Verified starting points

- TRAILS' [DRA016285 file archive](https://ddbj.nig.ac.jp/public/ddbj_database/dra/fastq/DRA016/DRA016285/) exposes experiment, sample and run XML plus 29 sequencing folders. The [human-data record](https://humandbs.dbcls.jp/research/hum0312/v2) lists unrestricted release. The subsets originate from one donor. Reads are amplified cDNA; sequencing-read totals are not automatically original-molecule counts. Our preceding audit examined annotations and assigned counts, not these raw reads.
- The alternative-ending TRAILS model is `e09f04f5-c617-4bb3-b69c-519ccb16d0f1`. It uses +29 at chr21:42434983→42437488 but then 42437580→42437855 and ends at 42440816. It is distinct from the boundary reference and ends before the stop used by the five earlier conditional +29 products.
- [PXD000376](https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/cgi/GetDataset?ID=PXD000376-1&test=no) supplies the completed bounded naïve-CD4/non-nuclear search. Its selected fourteen-file experiment contains 66,766 MS2 scans. All four target peptides lack retrieved-reference competitors and exported spectrum assignments; useful ordinary UBASH3A coverage was not demonstrated. Other experiments remain unsearched. The TRAILS-associated [PXD040962](https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/cgi/GetDataset?ID=PXD040962) instead contains LCL/THP-1 cell-line proteomics and would provide a separate transfer context.
- A literature accession needs reconciliation: the [resting/activated CD4 paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7795831/) cites PXD015872, whereas the [current registry entry](https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/cgi/GetDataset?ID=PXD015872) describes THP-1 macrophage differentiation. Do not classify those files as CD4 data without resolving this mismatch.
- The [existing PRKD2 results](../reports/qtl-followup.md) show agreement between the model's expression direction and measured BLUEPRINT/DICE monocyte associations. The completed [shared-signal analysis](../reports/prkd2-signal-analysis.md) reproduces those measurements but cannot establish a shared PSC–RNA causal signal because dominant variant evidence is absent from the comparisons.

## Fixed initial execution scope

Join the DRA016285 experiment, sample and run records explicitly, identify the naïve and memory CD4 libraries, and inventory their file sizes/checksums before retrieving the sequencing data. Freeze sample selection, coordinate conventions, reference completeness, aligner settings, artifact checks and evidence categories before inspecting target reads. Use all qualifying evidence, including normal splicing, alternate structures and low-confidence alignments; do not select a favorable RNA ending.

Begin with the two broad naïve/memory CD4 subsets if the metadata identify both uniquely. This is a bounded initial audit, not all 29 subsets or independent donor replication. Any expansion to other subsets is a separately documented stage. Primary genome alignment must consider competing loci; a gene-only alignment cannot establish genomic uniqueness. Local reference-only screening can nominate reads but must not be treated as the final mapping result.

All four initial computational scopes are now complete. Both complete RNA libraries passed source-total and decompression checks. The raw analysis did not confirm either the +29 junction or T29's distinguishing downstream junction. Direction 3 found no qualifying candidate protein evidence in its frozen experiment. Direction 4 remains inconclusive for a shared PSC–PRKD2 RNA cause after complete signal retrieval and full-allele recovery. Its report identifies the missing association statistics and sample-covariance inputs needed for a more informative comparison; no requests have been sent. The conditional sequence reconstruction does not substitute for observed RNA or protein evidence.

The separately requested [PRKD2 data-recovery follow-up](../reports/prkd2-public-data-recovery.md) is also complete within its finite scope. Original IBDverse classical/intermediate monocytes recover the missing SNP and clear marginal coverage, making them useful candidates for the next signal comparison. Exact original model/sample information and suitable study-matched LD or complete signal vectors remain necessary; no new shared-cause posterior is reported. The original four-stage results are unchanged.

The proposed [laboratory experiment](ubash3a-cd4-experiment-brief.md) remains unperformed. The computational work can improve structural, translation and genetic evidence. A decay claim still requires appropriate turnover measurements from an informative public experiment or new laboratory data.
