### 1. Liver: retain the actual narrow question

The [accepted method](psc-liver-program-independent-review.md) fixes **ten unsigned/as-source programs × three contrasts = 30 Core hypotheses**, with both BH and BY over that full family. ETS2 gRNA1-down, **PSC−AIH**, is focal. PSC−ASC and ASC−AIH are secondary; **17 PSC / 17 ASC / 30 AIH** source patient-samples stay separate. Strict identity is sensitivity, not replacement discovery.

Scores are positive equal-member mean `log2(CPM+1)`, using the full **41,096-row released RNA universe** for library totals. The complete official ECM source has **321 symbols**; it is unsigned membership, not a directional fibrosis-response signature. Missing members are not measured zeros. Neither gRNA1-up inversion nor a favorable mapped-subset denominator is allowed.

A positive contrast would support an **unadjusted whole-biopsy RNA association**. A null, opposite or imprecise contrast would limit transport claims in this pediatric disease-control setting. None identifies ETS2 dependence, fibrosis activity, a cell-intrinsic effect, genetic causality or treatment benefit. Cell composition, disease stage, treatment and unlinked clinical/batch covariates remain alternatives. The source-patient independence assumption is explicit, not independently verified person/visit disjointness. Different significance calls across programs do not test that one program is more specific than another.

The [closed MacroMap result](macromap-program-context.md) is a fixed-model predictive gain in healthy-derived iPSC macrophages. It is not unique ETS2 information or dependence; correlated predictors and fixed-ridge regularization remain possible explanations. This liver analysis is a different observational question, **not mechanistic confirmation of MacroMap**. It also does not complete the held epithelial IL17-response transfer.

### 2. BACH2: keep the one admitted RNA endpoint

The [prospective review](psc-bach2-followup-design-review.md) and root decision fix **BACH2 / ENSG00000112182**, the **C0/C1 naïve-CD4 union**, and eight original male PSC donors: **four source SNP versus four noSNP**. The 16,659 selected cells are not independent replicates. The sole endpoint is the equal-donor difference of `log2(1 + 10^6 × BACH2 units / all 36,601 RNA-feature units)` pooled within the same selected cells.

This deliberately leaves C0/C1 mixture and RNA-content weighting in the estimand. It does not reconstruct the missing later activation states or the unspecified historical Figure S2B normalization/test. A clear difference could qualify a broad RNA-similarity interpretation; a null or wide interval does not establish RNA equivalence or a translation-only mechanism. The same eight people are not independent validation, and there is no BACH2 protein/translation endpoint. Clinical imbalance and the missing participant-row bridge remain.

Only aggregates may be public. All eight donor quantities and donor-indexed omissions remain private. Complete the single nominal Welch contrast, fixed diagnostics, independent checks and mechanical replay only. Do not add genes, states, ADT proxies or a “tolerance” score after any outcome.

## Reserve 1 — GSE255234: PARK for value and identification

### Actual study, not the mistaken shorthand

The [closed source qualification](ets2-study-qualification.md) and [Pradhan et al. study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.redox.2024.103191) establish **heme**, not MEK inhibition:

- Four healthy human monocyte-derived macrophage donors, each with **CON, LPS, H10 and H5**: 16 libraries, four biological donor units.
- **H5 = 5 µM heme; H10 = 10 µM heme; LPS = 1 µg/mL**, each alone for **6 h**. H5 is fully identified, not an unresolved arm to discard.
- Differentiation uses 5% AB serum/25 ng/mL M-CSF; treatment uses 1% serum/12.5 ng/mL M-CSF. Human differentiation duration must not be inferred from the adjacent mouse method.
- CON is a source control, but exact harvest time and vehicle/solvent equivalence are not documented. Heme stock, culture/plate dates, randomization and library batches remain unqualified.

**GSE84161 is the separate MEK-pharmacology branch.** Its donor-block and array-dose problems do not belong to GSE255234.

Bulk RNA-seq uses TruSeq Stranded mRNA/NextSeq 500. Reported processing includes STAR 2.4.0.1 and HT-seq 0.11.1 against **Hg38 RefSeq**, whereas the previously qualified workbook has **64,253 unique ENSG rows × 16 columns**. The exact annotation/conversion and complete historical pre-filter count universe remain unknown. These source statements are preserved, not corrected by inference.

### Strongest single candidate considered, not adopted

A four-donor paired **H10−LPS at 6 h** contrast of the fixed ETS2 gRNA1-down program, retaining its existing ETS2 exclusion, could be defined using a **released-universe rank score**. One transparent example would use midranks over all 64,253 deposited rows, retaining zero ties, and one fixed mapping/coverage rule. This is a dimensionless within-release position, not absolute RNA, ETS2 activity or the MacroMap adjusted-log-score endpoint. No rank distribution, mapping coverage or gene value was inspected here. No method is frozen.

The contrast could equivalently be written `(H10−CON)−(LPS−CON)` under the same scoring rule: CON cancels. Thus unknown CON harvest is **not an algebraic blocker for an active-arm comparison**. It does not resolve active-treatment vehicle/batch differences, establish either baseline response, or make the doses biologically equipotent. H5 remains preserved; it is not an extra endpoint or a fallback if H10 disappoints.

The explicit source-study heme/LPS comparison would be reused. No source reuse in the original ETS2 program derivation was identified by the closed readiness audit. Primary hMDMs and healthy-derived iPSC macrophages are different experimental systems; this is real context extension, not an exact MacroMap replication. Individual cross-study donor disjointness was not established and no identity join was attempted.

### Why not run it now?

- **Higher under heme:** would show a relative score difference in these libraries, compatible with redox/stress responses, co-regulation or shifts elsewhere in the rank universe.
- **Higher under LPS:** would not establish ETS2 specificity, exclude heme responsiveness or contradict a classifier fitted in a different macrophage system.
- **Small or imprecise difference:** would not establish equality with four donors, nor distinguish both arms increasing from both decreasing relative to CON.

None of these outcomes identifies **ETS2 dependence**, explains the closed ridge-model gain, or determines the cause of a prospective PSC−AIH liver difference. There is no PSC factor, ETS2 intervention or risk-allele perturbation. Broad inflammatory/oxidative responses, potency, dynamics, viability and relative-RNA effects remain competing explanations. Testing an alleged ETS2-specific reporter would challenge a claim the completed reports expressly do not make.

**Separate the three judgments:** the old complete-universe CPM procedure remains unqualified; a separately frozen released-universe descriptive analysis is possible in principle; its present PSC decision value is low. The cached workbook is not inaccessible. A small computation would still require a new mapping/method/verification/reporting cycle for four donor contrasts. The **73,683,351,141-byte raw-read route is not justified or requested**. Reprocessing would create a new count pipeline, not recover an unknown historical one.

## Reserve 2 — GSE303012: PARK despite a useful factorial design

### What the cached source actually establishes

The [GEO metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE303012) describe **transformed H69 cholangiocytes**, four arms—Vehicle, TGFβ, Aramchol and TGFβ+Aramchol—with three sample labels each. This is **one cell line, not 12 patient donors**. Suffixes `_1`–`_3`, distinct BioSamples and ordered library numbers do not establish independent culture experiments or paired blocks.

Series exposure is **10 ng/mL TGFβ and 30 µM Aramchol acid, overnight**. Sample methods describe **six-hour serum starvation followed by serum-free treatment**. Six hours is not the specified drug exposure. Exact treatment hours, order/pretreatment, synchronized harvests, passage, batch and randomization are unverified. Vehicle samples exist; vehicle identity/concentration and matching across all arms are unresolved, not absent.

The source declares total-RNA extraction, NextSeq 2000, GRCh38 with Ensembl release-108 annotation, STAR 2.7.9a, featureCounts paired-read counting, and DESeq2 median-of-ratios normalization. Subread/DESeq2 versions and the executed historical environment are not supplied in the inspected metadata. Its ≥10-read/≥3-sample filter and DEG thresholds are reported methods, not a new analysis specification. The literal library-kit wording is retained in the source audit rather than silently repaired.

`GSE303012_combinedCounts.csv.gz` is publicly listed but **not acquired or payload-qualified**. File size/checksum, features, columns, count quantity, completeness and library QC remain unverified. This is conditional readiness, not a demonstrated HTTP failure. An explicit new released-universe analysis could be valid after qualification without reproducing every historical method.

### Strongest single candidate considered, not adopted

For **one independently fixed readout and scale**, the contrast

```text
(TGFβ+Aramchol − TGFβ) − (Aramchol − Vehicle)
```

can distinguish a TGFβ-conditioned drug response from a purely additive common drug shift **on that scale**. A count-model interaction instead represents a log ratio-of-ratios. Neither is absolute RNA. A negative interaction alone is not “reversal”: component directions and the readout's biological meaning matter. These are distinct from twelve independent donor responses or evidence of reversal after established injury; treatment order is unresolved.

The GEO summary already reports attenuation of TGFβ-related fibrosis pathways with Aramchol. This would reuse that source experiment, not independently validate the paper. **The inspected metadata do not establish whether the paper already fitted this exact interaction.** Its abstract/full text were not inspected here; the known PMID **40719557** and publisher link come from GEO. No article-access failure is claimed.

The missing element is not merely a count matrix. There is **no admitted independently fixed epithelial IL17-response endpoint** to transfer. The external IL17 source is held. The organoid's eight NB-BH/six BY candidates, with no corrected Welch discoveries, are model-dependent disease-by-IL17 interactions—not an automatically portable response signature. H69 has neither a PSC/nonPSC donor factor nor an IL17 arm, so changing expression of selected genes would not reproduce that interaction.

The official unsigned ECM set could support a separately defined transcript-abundance measurement. It cannot be relabeled a signed fibrosis/IL17 response. Here an ECM drug interaction would mostly refine the already reported TGFβ/Aramchol context, without identifying the remaining PSC mechanism. No broad gene/program screen is warranted to find a more attractive endpoint.

### What would an answer change?

A nonzero interaction could reject additive drug action at that one endpoint, but would remain compatible with TGFβ-dependent toxicity, survival/growth or RNA-composition changes and transformed-line/starvation context. A precisely small interaction would constrain nonadditivity there; an imprecise null would not. Neither proves IL17/PSC specificity. An opposite result could challenge a defined H69 attenuation prediction, **not** invalidate the organoid result or establish ETS2, SCD or BACH2 mechanisms.

Viability, cell number and target engagement are unmeasured in this audit, not proven absent from the source paper. Clear culture/vehicle provenance and payload qualification would improve readiness but **would not by themselves supply the missing PSC discriminator**. Arithmetic may be cheap; source and endpoint work dominate its limited present value. PARK, rather than build another same-study score analysis.
