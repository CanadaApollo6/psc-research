# PSC analysis frontier: the next finite set of biological tests

Reviewed **15 September 2026 UTC**. This is a prospective portfolio review, not a new effect analysis. The blood result is complete; the GSE239283 donor interaction is active and **no new organoid effects were read for this review**. Earlier source values, protocols and findings remain unchanged.

## Decision

**Continue after the organoid analysis. Run MacroMap next, then a fixed liver disease-control analysis.** Neither depends on obtaining a positive organoid interaction. Reserve *interaction-derived* liver transfer for an endpoint that passes a rule fixed before the organoid results.

The bounded search also found two substantive additions: an eight-donor PSC **BACH2/miR4464 genotype study with public processed-file manifests**, and a larger **Norwegian PSC liver atlas with cirrhotic disease controls**. Their design is useful, but donor/file joins or downloadable measurement provenance still need one bounded check. They are not already-qualified new replications. No new, immediately usable PSC GWAS or PSC-specific pQTL resource was established.

The completed blood analysis constrains this choice: **93 BH conjunction candidates, zero BY candidates, zero stronger disease-control conjunction candidates, and none of the seven fixed benchmarks passing the primary criterion**. These results do not justify a classifier, relaxed cutoffs or new model-score searches. Nor do they prove that PSC lacks a specific mechanism.

### 1. MacroMap: the strongest immediately executable next analysis

**Inputs.** [Zenodo 11563707](https://zenodo.org/records/11563707), the [expression paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61670-9), Supplementary Data 11 and the pinned original GENCODE v27 annotation. The [completed qualification](research-path-review.md#completed-bounded-macromap-qualification) establishes **58,243 gene rows × 4,698 samples**, 209 source lines and **205 distinct mapped HipSci identities**. It supports **169–193 mapped-line pairs** per strict contrast. The publisher describes unrelated healthy donors; this is not a new kinship check. GRCh38, STAR 2.5.3a and featureCounts 1.5.3 are declared. The count release excludes 45 annotation-only `_PAR_Y` records; do not manufacture their counts.

**Precise estimand.** For each unchanged program and each line, compute the stimulation-minus-time-matched-control score. Retain all **CIL, IFNB, IFNG, IL4, LIL10, MBP, P3C, R848 and sLPS**, each at **6 and 24 hours**. The two PIC rows remain unavailable for the strict endpoint: no separately identified mock-transfection arm is released. CIL/LIL10 are mixtures; MBP is myelin basic protein. Do not interpret these as isolated-component effects.

Use the already fixed ETS2 gRNA1-down program, excluding ETS2, its related guide/enhancer companions, and the four existing inflammation, interferon-γ, oxidative-stress and apoptosis comparators. Preserve the comparator-overlap-removal sensitivity and original mapping gates. Learn abundance-matched reference sets and scaling **only from training controls**. State that normalization uses the verified *released* feature universe, not a recovered historical full counting pipeline.

**Discriminating test.** In addition to the complete response panel, freeze one simple broad-program baseline and the same baseline plus ETS2 for predicting the nine stimulus identities from paired changes. Make time handling explicit, for example separate 6/24-hour evaluations with one fixed aggregate log-loss difference. Use a single model class and a finite regularization rule; any tuning stays inside training runs. Hold out whole sequencing runs within protocol strata. Weight lines equally and keep all conditions from a line together. Report protocol-specific results before any pooled summary. Resample runs, or use a declared run-aware line procedure; 18 conditions, thousands of genes and repeated samples are not independent donor replications.

**What it can change.** Reproducible incremental held-out information would support a useful *context score*, beyond these particular comparators. It would not establish ETS2 dependence, PSC specificity or treatment response. Failure to improve held-out loss, disagreement across protocols, loss of the pattern after overlap removal, or donor effects indistinguishable from broad responses would weaken that utility claim. A confidence interval including no gain is imprecision, not proof of exact redundancy.

**Independence and prior publication.** This is a different study from the original ETS2 perturbation and prior liver atlas. No MacroMap reuse was identified in the bounded Stankey-source check; that is not universal donor-disjointness certification. MacroMap already published stimulus-responsive expression/QTL biology, and its QC selected successfully stimulated samples. Neither stimulus responsiveness nor ETS2's inflammatory role is a new discovery here. The added question is fixed-program incremental information on held-out lines/runs.

**Compute and boundary.** About 4–8 CPU cores, 8–16 GB RAM and roughly 0.5–3 hours for matrix scoring and compact held-out models. Use the existing 174-MB compressed count file; no reads or eQTL archive are needed. Stop after the full frozen panel, protocol/holdout assessment and donor/run sensitivity. Do not search new signatures or stimuli to rescue weak performance.

### 2. Independent liver disease-control comparisons

**Inputs.** [GSE303271](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE303271) has a [3,021,812-byte count file](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE303nnn/GSE303271/suppl/GSE303271_raw_counts.txt.gz): **17 PSC, 17 ASC and 30 AIH** deposited labels. The [2026 paper](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.199226) describes 64 patient biopsies and groups PSC/ASC within its 34-PSC account. Retain the raw labels; do not silently turn 34 into an independent PSC-only sample count. GRCh38, STAR 2.7.2b and featureCounts 1.6.4 are declared.

[GSE159676](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE159676) has a [2,252,130-byte Affymetrix matrix](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE159nnn/GSE159676/matrix/GSE159676_series_matrix.txt.gz): **12 PSC, 7 NASH, 3 PBC, 3 AIH, 1 haemochromatosis, 1 alcohol-related and 6 control-style records**. The series says five controls; the sixth deposited record is not an invented extra donor. The tissue mixes explants and diagnostic biopsies from the Norwegian biobank. Pin the exact array annotation and measurement transform; do not pool array and sequencing values.

**Freeze two routes separately.**

- The unconditional question uses a small **externally defined** epithelial IL-17/injury program plus fixed broad inflammation, interferon, stress and fibrosis comparators. Its source/version, gene IDs, directions, weighting and mapping coverage must be fixed before tissue values. This can proceed even if the organoid interaction is weak.
- An interaction-derived program is a **discovery-trained transfer endpoint**. Fix its selection/weighting algorithm and trigger before organoid effects, then freeze the realized endpoint before liver effects. Do not tune it on the liver labels. If the interaction fails its trigger, do not call a relaxed gene list a validated PSC-specific response.

**Comparisons.** Primary pediatric contrast: PSC minus AIH; report PSC–ASC and ASC–AIH as fixed secondary contrasts. In adult tissue, use a fixed PSC-versus-non-PSC chronic-liver-disease mixture as the aggregate contrast, while also showing PSC–NASH, PSC–PBC and PSC–AIH separately. The mixture estimates a comparison to that exact etiology distribution, not “all liver disease.” The three-person PBC/AIH groups are imprecise; they must not be hidden inside a favorable pooled result. Disease versus healthy-style tissue is descriptive support, not the specificity test.

Use donor-level effects/intervals and a small complete endpoint family. Check biopsy identity, stage and batch overlap before fitting. If sample-linked fibrosis/clinical covariates are public, prespecify their use. Do not attach group-level table means to individuals. Total-tissue and composition-adjusted analyses are different estimands; inferred composition is not measured cell counts or proof of an intrinsic epithelial effect. A stage-confounded comparison stays observational even after a score adjustment.

**Falsifier and novelty.** Opposing cross-study directions, similar shifts in comparator diseases, or a result explained by broad fibrosis/composition would weaken disease specificity. Wide intervals do not demonstrate equal biology. The pediatric paper already reports macrophage–fibroblast programs and fibrosis associations; the adult paper already reports portal mesenchymal/fibrosis signatures across liver diseases. Repeating either is not a new finding. The incremental question is transport/specificity of an independently fixed response, not re-selection of their published genes.

The pediatric paper cites **GSE61260** as adult PSC validation; the earlier accession check found a liver age/BMI study without an established PSC cohort. Preserve this source discrepancy and **do not use that label as an independent PSC replication**. Its two-donor GSE325861 spatial deposit is the same study family and cannot provide population replication through spots or cells.

**Compute and stop.** 2–4 cores, 4–8 GB RAM, generally under an hour after annotation qualification; the two compressed expression files total about 5.3 MB. One input pass must establish donor identity, assay quantity and estimable groups. Otherwise report the affected contrast unavailable. Finish all fixed disease comparisons without searching a more favorable disease grouping or program.

The deposited count file was not downloaded; biological culture replication/batch pairing is not established merely by `_1`–`_3` suffixes.

If a locked organoid response merits an orthogonal specificity test, estimate the four-arm interaction:

`(TGFβ+Aramchol − TGFβ) − (Aramchol − vehicle)`

on a declared normalized program scale, or the corresponding count-model log ratio-of-ratios. Show both drug contrasts and both TGFβ contrasts. A drug shift in untreated cells alone is not selective reversal of the injury response. The [2025 source study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40719557/) already reports Aramchol attenuation of fibrosis/TGFβ programs. A repeated inhibition result would not discover a therapy. ## Independent critique of the active GSE239283 analysis

The proposed negative-binomial interaction, paired-change Welch, exhaustive label sensitivity and donor omission panel are useful **if their distinct targets and limits are explicit**.

1. **Scale and rank.** A donor-fixed-effects model can be written `log(mu_gdt) = log(size_factor_dt) + alpha_gd + beta_g*T + gamma_g*(PSC*T)`. Eight donor indicators plus the two treatment terms have rank 10 in 16 observations: **six residual degrees of freedom**. A separate disease main effect is redundant. `gamma` is a log **ratio of treatment ratios**, not a raw-count difference-in-differences. Report `beta`, `beta+gamma` and `gamma`: a positive interaction can also mean a smaller decrease in PSC.
2. **Measurement.** Integer entries alone do not prove original UMI counts; SCT-corrected counts can also be integers. Resolve the merged matrix's assay provenance before giving it to a count likelihood. Use a fixed all-epithelial donor × treatment population, not treatment-selected clusters. These are paired sister cultures, not repeated observations of the same cells. Library-normalized pseudobulk measures relative RNA composition and can include survival/state-composition changes, not absolute output per organoid.
3. **Equal-donor sensitivity.** Freeze a normalized log transform/pseudocount, calculate each donor's IL17–control change, and compare the four changes against the other four with Welch. This equal-donor estimand need not equal the NB estimate. With positive variances, Welch df lies between 3 and 6. Count depth does not compensate for eight donors. Neither agreement between methods nor multiple clusters supplies independent replication.
4. **Fixed BH/BY family.** Preserve all eligible genes, failures and source NA values; use 1 only inside declared correction bookkeeping if needed. BH relies on valid marginal P values and suitable dependence assumptions. BY tolerates arbitrary dependence, **not** invalid P values, misspecified counts or pseudoreplication. Small-sample NB calibration and dispersion borrowing remain assumptions. Check influence explicitly: unique donor-treatment design rows may defeat usual automatic Cook filtering. Do not choose the test that yields more discoveries.
5. **Seventy label assignments.** Enumerate all `choose(8,4)=70` assignments on whole donor changes, including the observed assignment and ties. For a two-sided absolute studentized statistic, complementary assignments match: **minimum P = 2/70 = 1/35**, not 1/70. The next attainable value exceeds 0.05. Consequently ordinary BH needs at least **4/7 of the entire family at the minimum P** to reject anything; BY cannot reject for any family of at least three tests at 0.05. These permutations are a coarse donor-label sensitivity, **not a practical transcriptome-wide BH/BY validation gate**. This arithmetic does not constrain the continuous NB P values.
6. **Exchangeability is not established.** PSC status is not randomized. Age, clinical indication, sampling site/method, cirrhosis and culture history can affect responsiveness. Donor intercepts remove time-invariant levels, not those factors' treatment interactions. Equal mean response alone does not imply exchangeable change distributions; studentization is not a finite-n cure. Enumerating all labels does not turn this into a randomized disease experiment. Swapping treatment labels within each pair tests a different null when a common treatment response exists.
7. **Leave one donor out.** Remove complete pairs, retain the frozen feature family, and state whether normalization/dispersion are refitted. Show all eight estimates/signs and unavailable fits. The seven-donor fits have five residual df. Their range is not a confidence interval; they are not eight replications. Do not demand individual significance in every omission or drop an influential donor after inspecting the effects.

**Next-step rule.** Before organoid effects, choose either a small externally defined program trigger with its own fixed multiplicity rule, or a gene-derived trigger with the full-family primary rule, adequate count support, no unresolved fit failure, agreement with equal-donor direction and direction stability across complete-donor omissions. Report BY separately; do not require the arithmetically impossible permutation-BY validation. Freeze exact transfer genes/directions/weights and liver source choice before liver effects. This is permission for an independent test, not a discovery certification. Do not pick whichever trigger happens to pass.

If the interaction is weak, the correct result is **“not detected under this eight-donor design and these culture conditions,”** with interval precision and unavailable measurements stated. It is not equality, absence of an IL-17 response, proof of no PSC role or evidence against a treatment. A shared IL-17 response may still be measured. Only a prospectively meaningful equivalence margin with adequate interval precision could support equivalence.

**Already published:** [Garcia Moreno et al.](https://doi.org/10.1097/HC9.0000000000000454) explicitly reports different PSC/non-PSC IL-17 responses. The added work is donor-aware re-estimation, complete-family uncertainty and influence assessment, not discovery of that hypothesis or independent organoid replication. Public methods do not establish that the authors never considered donor dependence; avoid alleging an undocumented historical error.

## Defensible stopping boundary

“Exhaust all science” is not an attainable completion criterion. A defensible endpoint is **one complete result for each executable discriminating question, plus explicit unavailable outcomes for the remaining finite queue**.

- Finish the organoid analysis, MacroMap panel and qualified liver comparisons even if they are negative. A negative result is not permission to reopen a frozen endpoint or mine the held-out samples.
- Allow one bounded input pass for each new resource. Necessary facts are donor identity, measured assay quantity, comparison structure and sufficient coverage—not perfect reconstruction of every historical software detail. If a new estimand is valid despite a historical reproduction gap, declare it separately rather than pretending the old gate passed.
- Keep GSE255234 as reserve: all four donors and three heme/LPS arms are known, but its historical full-universe CPM gate remains unpassed. A new available-universe rank analysis is possible only under its own frozen question; do not spend roughly 73.7 GB of read transfer just to avoid that distinction.
- Keep PRKD2 parked until genuinely new suitable signal/covariance/sample information arrives. Do not generate more shared-cause posteriors from unchanged insufficient inputs. Keep UBASH3A complete RNA/protein and ETS2 direct-dependence claims tied to informative measurements or appropriate perturbation, not another structure/model run.
- Stop a branch when the next discriminating evidence requires unavailable donor labels/counts, authorized access/contact, suitable laboratory validation, or an independent cohort that has not been released. Record the exact missing input and a concrete reopening trigger. Repeating the same search or relabeling reused donors does not meet that trigger.
- If the finite queue yields only generic context effects, imprecise interactions or non-transfer, report that bounded conclusion. The proper next step may be a new public release or a separately authorized experiment. A larger model cannot replace the missing biological comparison.

**Action now:** complete the active donor interaction; then execute MacroMap and the prespecified bulk liver disease-control question. Qualify the two new higher-value resources once, without allowing those checks to delay the already executable analyses.
