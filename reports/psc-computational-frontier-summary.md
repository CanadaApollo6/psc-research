RNA/array inputs are **measurements**. Blood, liver and genotype-group contrasts are observational. Organoid exposure is experimental, but disease groups were not randomized. MacroMap predicts stimuli from RNA; it does not perturb ETS2. Published functional/protein experiments remain source evidence, not new experiments here. Sequence/structure predictions are not measured function or clinical outcomes.

Nulls do not establish equivalence or absence of biology. Composite scores can miss cell-restricted or offsetting changes; this work does not show that either occurred. Donors—not cells, genes, omissions or replays—supply biological replication. No breakthrough, diagnostic utility or treatment benefit is established.

## Leads, not a new queue

Reopening requires evidence, as in the [accepted appraisal](psc-remaining-frontier-appraisal.md):

- **Organoid/ETS2:** independent donor perturbation, matched controls and functional/target-engagement evidence; a stress score cannot establish ETS2 dependence.
- **PRKD2:** executed sample/covariate/model records and suitable signed RNA/PSC covariance or complete signal vectors. Marginal statistics and clumps are insufficient.
- **UBASH3A:** full RNA splice-chain, stability and isoform-specific protein evidence in relevant cells. Conditional translation is not an observed product; prior peptide nondetection lacked a qualifying ordinary UBASH3A control.
- **Held sources:** a complete traceable epithelial IL17 response definition; corrected/reconstructed GSE159676 scale and person/biopsy provenance (**six PSC people, not twelve**); NoPSC bulk-export/donor/overlap qualification; GSE84161 donor-block and array-dose confirmation.
- **Models:** new AlphaGenome/AlphaFold inference is **unverified in the checked setup**, not permanently or globally unavailable. Authorized access/runtime, exact inputs and a justified bounded question are needed. Public AFDB metadata are existing predictions, not new folding. The UBASH3A folding proposal also needs product evidence.

**GSE255234 heme/LPS and GSE303012 H69 remain PARKED for limited incremental discrimination, not access failure.** An explicit heme released-universe contrast or H69 fixed-scale drug×TGFβ nonadditivity test could be valid. Neither presently resolves the missing PSC evidence. No raw-read shortcut, signature sweep or rescue analysis follows.
